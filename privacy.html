PRIVACY POLICY

zen dashboard is a personal tool used only by its owner, on the owner's own computer.

what it accesses

  • Your YouTube liked videos, to show the liked songs list.
  • The ability to like or unlike a video, only when you click the heart.

where your data goes

Nowhere else. The sign-in token and the liked songs list are stored only on the owner's computer. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's own YouTube API, and nothing is shared, sold or used for advertising.

how to remove access

Remove "zen dashboard" at myaccount.google.com/permissions at any time; the saved token stops working immediately.
